Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

The short version

Rowline has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your patterns, projects and notes stay on your iPad. The only thing that leaves your iPad is an anonymous record of your Rowline Pro purchase, used to unlock and restore Pro.

What stays on your iPad

The PDF patterns you import, your projects, counters, reminders and Apple Pencil notes are stored only in Rowline's storage on your device. Rowline never uploads them anywhere.

Purchases

Rowline Pro is sold through Apple's App Store, and Apple handles the payment. Rowline never sees your payment details.

To confirm and restore your purchase, Rowline uses RevenueCat, a purchase-validation service. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID and the record of your Rowline purchases (for example, which product you bought and when). It doesn't receive your name, email address or payment details. This information is used only to provide and restore your purchase. It isn't used for advertising or tracking, and it isn't sold. RevenueCat protects it at least as well as described in this policy.

Crash reports

If you have agreed to share analytics with app developers in your iPad's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Rowline itself sends no other diagnostics.

Keeping and deleting data

Your patterns, projects and notes are deleted when you delete them in Rowline or delete the app. The anonymous purchase record is kept so you can restore Rowline Pro. To have it deleted, email us at m.agahcicek@gmail.com.

Children

Rowline doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page, with the date it was updated.

Contact
