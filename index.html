Rowline Support

Rowline is an iPad app that follows your knitting and crochet PDF patterns row by row, so you never lose your place.

Contact us

Getting started

  1. Import a PDF pattern from Files, or tap Try the Sample Pattern on the empty library.
  2. A new project opens in setup mode. Scroll to your chart or instructions and tap Move Ruler Here.
  3. Drag the band's top and bottom handles onto one row. Pinch to zoom in for a precise fit, then tap Done.
  4. Tap Next Row as you knit. The band moves one row and the row counter follows. A keyboard or page-turner pedal works too (Down Arrow, Right Arrow, Space or Page Down).

Frequently asked questions

Does Rowline change my PDF?
No. Your pattern file is never changed. Your ruler, counters and Apple Pencil notes are saved with your project, separately from the file.

How do I move the ruler to another page?
Scroll to the page, then choose Move Ruler Here from the More (…) menu or the Counters and Ruler panel.

How do reminders work?
Open the Counters and Ruler panel and add a reminder such as "Decrease every 6 rows, starting at 6". Rowline shows it on exactly those rows.

What does Rowline Pro include?
Rowline Pro is a one-time purchase. It lets you work on as many projects at once as you like and export a PDF copy with your Pencil notes. The free version includes one active project with every feature of the row ruler, counters, reminders and notes.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPad?
Open Rowline, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Rowline Pro with. Family Sharing is supported.

Where is my data?
Your patterns, projects and notes stay on your iPad. Rowline has no accounts, ads or usage analytics. See the privacy policy.
